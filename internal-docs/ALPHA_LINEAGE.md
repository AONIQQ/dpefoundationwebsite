# Alpha Lineage publication

Prepared November 13, 2026. Route: `/alpha-lineage`.

## Sources

- `Alpha Directory low res up to the 100th Line.PDF`: 124-page source. The first eight pages (title, charter members, and three early initiations) are reproduced as native webpage sections. The retained 116-page directory is published as `/alpha-lineage/alpha-directory-first-through-100th-line.pdf` and begins with the First Line (source page 9), initiated December 5, 1920. All retained page content streams and extracted text match the corresponding source pages.
- `Alpha Directory low res since the 100th Line.PDF`: 221-page original, published unchanged as `/alpha-lineage/alpha-directory-since-100th-line.pdf`. Begins with the 101st Line and ends with the 207th.
- `Alpha Chronology brought up to date.eml`: forwarded by Albert Grasso on November 13, 2026, containing Terrence J. Boyle's updated 206th–212th Line rosters and seven inline photographs. The email container and correspondence headers are not published.

## Content handling

`content/alpha-lineage.json` holds the recent rosters, seasons, counts, and photo paths. Names, diacritics, scripts, and order follow the email. At Andrew’s request, capitalized surnames are displayed in normal name capitalization, including McInnis, von Brandt, and Turkish Yalçın. Standalone trailing line-number artifacts (`207`, `208`, and `2`), stray trailing commas, and excess whitespace are removed; spellings are not inferred or rewritten.

Photo associations follow the email's HTML heading/image placement, not filenames. In particular, `210th Line.PNG` is placed under the **211th Line** heading in the source email and is published as `line-211.png`. `210th Line a.jpeg` accompanies the 210th Line. Other images follow the corresponding 206th–209th and 212th headings.

Counts checked against the source: 206th 1; 207th 13; 208th 11; 209th 2; 210th 14; 211th 18; 212th 8. Total 67 names. The recent chronology overlaps the historical directory intentionally, supplying updated rosters through spring 2026.

Both PDF read links open the corresponding documents in a new tab; download links save the same files. The first directory is the 116-page First-through-100th-Line edition; the second directory is unchanged. Each recent photo opens its original file. Header and footer link to Alpha Lineage; the expanded header uses its mobile menu below 1536px to keep navigation from crowding the logo.

## Opening material reproduced on the webpage

`content/alpha-lineage-founders.json` preserves all 56 early member records: 20 charter members, 15 from the first initiation, 19 from the second, and two from the third. The charter-member section distinguishes the January 25, 1920 group from Samuel Colcord Bartlett, initiated November 13, 1920. The three early initiation dates are February 21, May 1, and May 22, 1920; these are distinct from the numbered First Line of December 5.

All 56 individual portraits are rendered directly from the source PDF's portrait areas and mapped in the original roster order. Native captions use full names in normal capitalization. The responsive grid uses two columns on phones, four on small screens, and six on larger screens; portraits keep their original proportions. Full names and birth/death records are native expandable text with normal surname capitalization. Missing or placeholder dates display as “Not recorded”; approximate recorded dates remain approximate. The cover's subject is represented by the page title and introduction rather than another cover-page image. Original source PDFs remain untouched in Downloads.
